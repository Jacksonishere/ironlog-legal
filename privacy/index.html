IronLog privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026

IronLog stores everything you enter on your device: workouts, body weight, measurements and progress photos. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

These are used only to take or import progress photos. Photos are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.

Apple Health (iOS) and Health Connect (Android)

With your permission, IronLog reads your body weight to show it on your weight chart. It never writes to Health or Health Connect. Health data is never shared, sold or used for advertising. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or Health Connect.

On-device AI

Routine suggestions run on your device's own model (Apple Intelligence on iOS, Gemini Nano on Android). Your prompts don't leave the device.

Exercise library

IronLog downloads exercise descriptions and images from a public exercise database. These requests contain no information about you or your training.

Backups

On iOS, IronLog's data can be included in your iCloud or computer backup, which is stored in your own Apple account and isn't accessible to us. On Android, IronLog's data is excluded from device backups. You can also export your data yourself from Profile → Data & Privacy.

Deleting your data

Use Profile → Data & Privacy → Erase all data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything IronLog stored on the device.

Children

IronLog is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
