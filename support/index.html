IronLog support

Questions, bugs or feature requests

Email jacksondevsupport@gmail.com. Include your phone model and OS version if you're reporting a bug.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. IronLog has no accounts and no servers, so we can't recover data for you. Export a backup from Profile → Data & Privacy to keep a copy.

Body weight isn't syncing

Check that IronLog has read access to body weight in the Health app (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), then reopen IronLog. Sync runs when the app opens.
